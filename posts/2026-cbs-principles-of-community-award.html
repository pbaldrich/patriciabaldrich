Honored with the 2026 CBS Principles of Community Award

November 13, 2026

← All posts
November 13, 2026 · exRNAlab

I am honored to have received a 2026 Principles of Community Award from the UC Davis College of Biological Sciences, recognizing contributions to building a more connected, welcoming, and supportive academic community.

Throughout my career, I have believed that science thrives when people have opportunities to connect, exchange ideas, support one another, and feel that their contributions matter. At the UC Davis Genome Center, I have been fortunate to turn this belief into action through initiatives such as our Faculty Talks, Mentoring Seminar Series, Genome Center Hive Users Group, annual Halloween Symposium, and Genome Center Awards.

These initiatives have different goals, but they share a common purpose: bringing people together, creating opportunities for professional growth, and recognizing the many contributions that make scientific research possible.

I am particularly grateful to Blake Meyers for his nomination and continued support, and to the many colleagues who help make these initiatives successful. Community building is never the work of one person. It takes people willing to share their time, expertise, ideas, and enthusiasm.

Congratulations also to fellow recipients Marty Usrey and Evan White for their important contributions to mentoring, accessibility, and inclusion.

This recognition is a meaningful reminder that supporting people and strengthening our scientific communities are essential parts of our work as scientists.

Read more about the award and the 2026 recipients in the UC Davis College of Biological Sciences announcement.
