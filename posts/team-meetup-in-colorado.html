Team Meetup in Colorado!

October 10, 2025

← All posts
October 10, 2025 · exRNAlab

Earlier this week, we met with our collaborators from the exRNA project in beautiful Colorado for a few days of science, brainstorming, and fresh mountain air. It was such a joy to catch up in person, share our latest results, and plan the next round of experiments together.

A huge thank you to Roger Innes and his team from Indiana University for making it such a fun and productive gathering! We even found time to explore the mountains around Boulder. The trees, lakes, and scenery were absolutely breathtaking.
