Tanner Smith

Junior Specialist

← All people

Tanner is a Junior Specialist in the Baldrich Lab. He earned his BA in Biology at Washington University in St. Louis in 2024. In the lab he works on studying extracellular transport of small RNAs, plant-microbe interactions in simulated microgravity, and sRNA involvement in the neurodevelopment of zebrafish. Outside the lab he enjoys hiking, climbing, fishing, and game nights with friends.
