New Article: Making Conferences in the Plant Sciences More Inclusive

September 1, 2025

← All posts
September 1, 2025 · exRNAlab

I’m excited to share a new article I co-authored, published in eLife, titled “Making conferences in the plant sciences more inclusive through community recommendations.” This work grew out of the ROOT & SHOOT Research Coordination Network, where a diverse group of colleagues came together to identify barriers and propose concrete steps toward equity, accessibility, and inclusion in our scientific meetings. We highlight recommendations on:

Developing community agreements that promote positive, welcoming behaviors; establishing reporting structures and ombudsperson support to ensure accountability; making conference site selection and accessibility more transparent and inclusive; and ensuring diverse speaker selection and equitable programming.

It was an honor to serve as corresponding author and to collaborate with such a dedicated team of colleagues. We hope these recommendations will serve as a practical resource for conference organizers across disciplines working to create safer, more inclusive spaces for all.

You can read the full article here: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.106877
